Adding Harbor System to your Discord server uses Discord's standard bot authorization flow. Once authorized, Harbor System joins your server and is ready for configuration. The process takes less than two minutes and requires you to have the Manage Server permission on the target server.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Invite Link">
    Go to `[HARBOR_INVITE_URL]` in your browser. This is the official Harbor System invite link. Make sure you are logged in to Discord in your browser before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Server">
    In the Discord authorization dialog, choose the server you want to protect from the dropdown menu. Only servers where you have the Manage Server permission will appear in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Permissions">
    Harbor System requests the permissions it needs to function. Review the listed permissions carefully before accepting. See [Permissions](/getting-started/permissions) for a full breakdown of what each permission is used for and the impact of removing it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the Bot">
    Click **Authorize** and complete the CAPTCHA if prompted. Discord uses this step to confirm the authorization is being made by a human.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Bot Joined">
    Check your server's member list or audit log for **Harbor System** joining. You will also see a system message in your default channel confirming the bot has been added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Installation

Harbor System is now a member of your server, but it is not yet configured or actively protecting anything. All security modules are disabled by default until you complete the initial setup. Follow the Quick Start guide to get your core security modules enabled in under 10 minutes.

<Note>
  Harbor System requires specific permissions to protect your server. Do not remove permissions after installation, as this will disable security features.
</Note>
