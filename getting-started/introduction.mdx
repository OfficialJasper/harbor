Harbor System is a security bot for Discord servers that protects your community from raids, nukes, spam, and malicious bots. This documentation walks you through what Harbor System does and how to get it running on your server.

## What Harbor Does

Harbor combines several protection layers in a single bot:

- **Anti-raid** — detects and stops coordinated join attacks before they disrupt your server.
- **Anti-nuke** — blocks mass channel deletions, role changes, and other destructive admin actions.
- **Anti-spam** — filters flood messages, invite links, and repeated content.
- **Verification** — requires new members to prove they are human before gaining access.
