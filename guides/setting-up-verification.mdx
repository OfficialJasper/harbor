Member verification is one of the most effective tools for preventing raids and bot infiltration. By adding a friction step between joining and gaining full channel access, you stop automated bots, AFK raiders, and low-effort bad actors before they ever reach your community. Most raids rely on speed and volume — verification breaks both.

## How Verification Works

When a new member joins your server, Harbor System immediately restricts them to a single verification channel. From there, they must complete a challenge before receiving the verified role. Once they pass, Harbor assigns the configured role and the member gains access to your standard channels. If they do not complete verification within the configured timeout window, Harbor automatically kicks them.

The full flow looks like this:

1. Member joins → Harbor assigns `@Unverified` role automatically
2. Member can only see and interact with `#verify-here`
3. Member completes the verification challenge
4. Harbor assigns the `@Member` role and removes or overrides the `@Unverified` role
5. Member now has access to all channels available to `@Member`

## Discord Role Setup

Before running Harbor's verification commands, configure the required roles and permissions in Discord's server settings. These are manual Discord-side steps.

1. **Create an "Unverified" role** with no permissions enabled. Do not grant it access to any channels by default.
2. **Configure `#verify-here`** to allow `@everyone` to read the channel but restrict interaction (sending messages or using components) to `@Unverified` only. This prevents verified members from cluttering the channel.
3. **Lock all other channels** so `@Unverified` cannot read them. Go through each channel's permission overrides and explicitly deny Read Messages for `@Unverified`, or remove the `@everyone` read permission and grant it only to `@Member`.
4. **Create a "Member" role** that has access to your general channels. This is the role Harbor will assign once verification is passed.

## Harbor Verification Commands

With your Discord roles and permissions in place, configure Harbor's verification module:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Verification">
    Turn on the verification module:

    ```
    /harbor verify enable
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Verification Mode">
    Choose how members complete the challenge. See the [Choosing a Verification Mode](#choosing-a-verification-mode) section below for guidance:

    ```
    /harbor verify mode:reaction
    ```

    Accepted values: `reaction`, `button`, `captcha`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Verification Channel">
    Point Harbor to the channel where new members will verify:

    ```
    /harbor verify channel:#verify-here
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Verified Role">
    Specify the role Harbor assigns to members who pass verification:

    ```
    /harbor verify role:@Member
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Unverified Role">
    Specify the role Harbor assigns to members immediately on join, before they verify:

    ```
    /harbor verify unverified-role:@Unverified
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Timeout">
    Configure how long a member has to complete verification before Harbor kicks them:

    ```
    /harbor verify timeout:10
    ```

    The value is in minutes. Members who do not verify within this window are automatically kicked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing a Verification Mode

| Mode | Best For | Friction Level |
|---|---|---|
| `reaction` | Small to mid-size communities with low bot risk | Low |
| `button` | General use, modern Discord UI feel | Low |
| `captcha` | Public servers or servers with high bot join risk | Medium |

Start with `reaction` or `button` for most servers. Upgrade to `captcha` if you notice bots still passing verification or if your server is publicly advertised in a space likely to attract automated accounts.

<Warning>
  Ensure your Unverified role truly cannot access any channel except the verify channel. Any misconfiguration will allow unverified members to bypass the restriction.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Pair a short timeout (5–10 minutes) with verification to auto-kick AFK joiners, keeping your member list clean.
</Tip>
